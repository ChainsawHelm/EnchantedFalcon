# Hardware Shopping List

## Required

### iPod Classic 5.5G (30GB or 80GB model)
- **Why 5.5G specifically:** Best Rockbox support, Wolfson WM8758 DAC (better audio than later models), well-documented hardware
- **Where to buy:** eBay, Facebook Marketplace, Swappa — typically $50-100 for a working unit
- **Model numbers:** A1136 (30GB), A1136 (80GB)
- **How to identify 5.5G vs 5G:** 5.5G has "Search" in the main menu; serial starts with V2 or later

### iFlash SD Adapter
- **iFlash Solo** (~$30) — single SD card slot, simplest option
- **iFlash Quad** (~$50) — four SD card slots, up to 2TB total
- **Why:** Replaces the spinning hard drive — faster transfers, no moving parts, much better battery life, more storage
- **Where to buy:** iflash.xyz (official), eBay

### SD Card
- **Samsung EVO Select 256GB** or similar — plenty for a huge music library
- **512GB** if you have a large FLAC collection
- Format as FAT32 (Rockbox requirement for iPod Classic)

## Optional but Recommended

### Replacement Battery
- **Cameron Sino 3000mAh** or similar iPod Classic replacement battery (~$15)
- **Why:** Original batteries are 20+ years old and degraded. Fresh battery + iFlash = 30+ hours playback
- **Where to buy:** Amazon, eBay

### Opening Tools
- **iFixit iPod Classic toolkit** or metal spudgers
- **Why:** The iPod Classic case is clipped together and notoriously hard to open without proper tools
- Tip: Start at the bottom edge, work up the sides

### USB Cable
- **30-pin to USB-A cable** (or 30-pin to USB-C with adapter)
- Needed for: initial Rockbox install, flashing firmware, syncing music
